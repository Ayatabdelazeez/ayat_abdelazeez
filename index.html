About Me

Turning messy data into reliable results, one documented step at a time.

Hello, I'm Ayat Abd Elazeez, a Data Engineer who helps startups and small business owners collect, clean, and structure their data. I'm an engineering student in Systems & Computers at Al-Azhar University, currently training in data engineering with the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI).
